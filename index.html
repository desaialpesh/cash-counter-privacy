1. About Cash Counter

Cash Counter is a mobile utility that helps users manually count banknotes and coins and calculate cash totals. The core counting and history features are designed to work offline. The app does not require users to create an account.

2. Cash-counting information stored on your device

Cash amounts, denomination quantities, count drafts, saved count history and app preferences are stored locally on your device to provide the app's counting and history features. The developer does not operate a Cash Counter cloud service to upload or synchronise these cash records.

Cash-counting records are not intended to be sent to Firebase Analytics or advertising services. You can delete records using the app's available controls. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored information. Device backup is disabled by the app's current configuration; actual behaviour should be verified on supported Android versions before release.

3. Analytics — optional and opt-in

Cash Counter is planned to use Google Firebase Analytics to understand basic app usage and improve the app. Analytics collection is disabled by default and should start only after you explicitly opt in. If you decline or later turn analytics off in the app's privacy settings, analytics collection should remain disabled.

The planned analytics events are limited to basic usage, such as app opens, screen views and count completion. The app is designed not to send cash totals, denomination quantities, saved history, user-entered descriptions or account/contact details as analytics event data. Firebase Analytics and its SDK may also process technical and app-instance information as described in Google's documentation and applicable settings.

Turning analytics off stops future collection by the app; it does not necessarily delete information already processed by Google. For information about Google's handling of data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

4. Advertising and consent

Cash Counter is planned to use Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and related services may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers where available, app interactions, diagnostics, ad delivery and measurement information. The exact information processed can depend on SDK versions, device settings, consent choices and applicable law.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is planned to present and manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices. Ad requests should be made only when the UMP SDK indicates that requests are permitted. Where a user does not consent to personalised advertising, an eligible non-personalised or limited-ad option may be used only where permitted by Google's SDK and applicable requirements. If advertising cannot be requested or loaded, the core counting features should remain available.

You can revisit advertising privacy choices through the app's privacy settings when the UMP privacy-options entry point is required or available. For more information, review the Google advertising information and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Information sharing and service providers

The developer does not sell locally stored cash-counting records. Google may process information through Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads and UMP to provide analytics, advertising, consent management, security, fraud prevention and related services, as applicable. These services are governed by Google's terms and privacy documentation. No Cash Counter account, cloud database or cash-record synchronisation service is planned for this release.

6. Internet access

An internet connection may be used by Google services for analytics, advertising and consent management. The app's core cash-counting functionality is designed to remain usable without an internet connection. Network access does not mean that cash-counting records are uploaded.

7. Data retention and deletion

Cash-counting records stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android and device behaviour. Analytics and advertising information processed by Google is subject to the relevant service settings, retention policies and available deletion controls. Disabling analytics or changing advertising consent does not necessarily erase information already processed by those services.

8. Children's privacy

Before publication: the developer must confirm the app's intended audience, Google Play target-audience declaration and whether the app is directed to children. This section must be updated to match those decisions and applicable law. Do not rely on this draft as a final statement about children.

9. International availability

Cash Counter may be distributed in multiple countries. Privacy and advertising requirements vary by location. Where required, applicable consent and privacy choices will be presented. The availability and type of advertising may vary according to location, consent and service eligibility.

10. Security

Cash-counting records are stored locally and are subject to the security of your device and operating system. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Google services apply their own security measures to information they process; consult the relevant Google documentation for details.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its SDKs, data practices or applicable requirements change. The final published version will show its effective date and last-updated date.
